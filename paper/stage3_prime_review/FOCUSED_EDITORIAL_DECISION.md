# Focused Editorial Decision - Revision Round 1

**Manuscript:** Target-specific sparse correction certificates recover stochastic language trajectories across numerical precision  
**Review stage:** Stage 3' focused re-review  
**Decision date:** 2026-07-23  
**Decision:** **Major Revision**

Dear Author(s),

The revision has substantially improved the manuscript. The central result is now framed as target-specific stochastic trajectory replay rather than a broad steganographic, semantic-quality or cross-hardware claim. Matched storage and direct-mechanism baselines distinguish sparse recording from the integer probability contract, and the new apportionment analysis supplies a valid per-step total-variation bound without inventing a distribution-free KL guarantee. The trust, state, compute and privacy assumptions are also materially clearer.

The revised evidence supports a credible specialist ML-systems research contribution. In particular, the matched unquantized top-two comparison shows a small but measurable sparsity advantage for the disclosed probability contract on the frozen stack. The paper appropriately presents this as bounded evidence, not a universal optimum.

Major Revision remains necessary under the original review contract. The independent physical-target replay requested by all balanced first-round reviewers has not been performed. The requested `(q,T,B,k)` sensitivity is only partially covered. In addition, the Methods section still names exact equality as the primary outcome, which conflicts with the revised claim that correction density is the empirical primary endpoint and exactness is an integrity gate. Finally, the immutable archive and author declarations are incomplete.

No new foundational defect was found. The remaining scientific issues are finite and actionable; author metadata and DOI work are submission-administration blockers rather than evidence against the bounded mechanism result. A second revision should be focused on these residual items and should not reopen the full first-round review.

Sincerely,

Stage 3' Focused Editorial Review
